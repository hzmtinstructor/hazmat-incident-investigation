Incident Overview

You are the first-due officer at a commercial/industrial building where a dense white/gray vapor cloud is venting from a doorway. The product is unknown, with possible chemical reaction, fire, toxic exposure, and explosive-atmosphere hazards.

Scenario priorities: Isolate → Identify → Notify → Mitigate. Use remote reconnaissance, maintain safe distances, protect people, and avoid interior commitment until the hazard is identified.
